SHIP LAYOUT
Collision

Unit shapes — scale them to size. Green, never exported to ship.glb: they are what the runtime hands to Havok. On the staging area they build a module's hull; on the ship they are one-off blockers belonging to a room.

Compounds

One reusable object made of several modules — a wall with its lamp, a doorway with its frame. Saved as a recipe, not a mesh: placing one expands it into ordinary elements that move together. A red × on a tile forgets the recipe; copies already in the ship are left alone.

Settings

Global authoring, lighting, probe and collision defaults.

Editor

The authoring view. None of this reaches the game — it is how you see what you are building, not how the game will look. It is still saved with the ship, so you come back to the view you left.

Runtime

What the game does with the ship: its lighting and material settings, saved in the manifest and read by the runtime, and the behaviours its elements carry. The lighting rows only take effect in the Runtime view — that is the rig they belong to.

Save compound

Files what is on the bench in the palette as one reusable object. Saving over a name that already exists replaces the recipe — copies already placed in the ship are ordinary elements and are left exactly as they are unless you ask for them to be rebuilt.

Chunks

A chunk is one room or corridor: the unit the portal renderer draws and culls by, and the unit a light's scope is measured in. Elements carry the chunk they belong to; this is where the names live.

Environment probes

Each box or sphere owns one local cubemap, independently from chunks. Cyan marks the projection-volume centre; orange marks the point it is captured from. Only geometry intersecting the volume is rendered into the cubemap, and elements carrying a probeExcluded, dynamic, liquefiable or weapon behaviour are left out of it. Every probe is captured at the cubemap size set in Settings, which is one size for the whole ship. The influence volumes are a separate question: violet marks where this cubemap fades out, and the red volume inside it where it is used at full strength. All three are pickable: drag to move, Ctrl+wheel to resize. The red box has no centre of its own — it is always concentric with the violet one — so it only resizes.

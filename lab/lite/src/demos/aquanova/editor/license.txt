Assets used by this editor
==========================

The editor itself bundles no models. It reads the Quaternius sci-fi kits from
the **BabylonAssets** repository — either a local checkout of it or
`https://assets.babylonjs.com`, whichever `tool/config.json` selects. Each kit
folder there carries its own `license.txt`; read that one, not this file, before
using any of those models.

    https://assets.babylonjs.com/kits/

The only asset that lives here is `env/bank_vault_2k.hdr`, the image-based
lighting the viewport uses in unbaked mode.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Do not take the kits out of BabylonAssets
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Those folders are a working copy for this project, and they mix models from
Quaternius' **$10/month PRO subscription** in with the free ones, unmarked. If
you want these kits for something of your own, get them from the author:

    https://quaternius.com/

That is a request about where you obtain the files, not a restriction on top of
the CC0 dedication.

permission and without attribution.

    https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Attribution is not required, but is deserved:

    Models by @Quaternius — https://www.patreon.com/quaternius

Only the `glTF/` variant of each pack is kept — the editor loads glTF, and the
Blender, FBX, OBJ, Godot and engine-project variants would add hundreds of
megabytes it never reads.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
env/
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The HDRI in `env/` is **not** distributed with the editor and is not covered by
the dedication above. The one sitting there locally is a placeholder with no
recorded provenance, so it is gitignored rather than committed; a licensed
replacement will take its place. The editor starts without it — metals simply
render flat, since they take nearly all their brightness from the IBL.

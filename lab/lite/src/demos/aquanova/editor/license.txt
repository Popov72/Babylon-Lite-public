Assets bundled with this editor
===============================

The `kit/` folder is the glTF export of the **Modular SciFi MegaKit** by
Quaternius, bundled here so the editor runs on any machine without a separate
download or a purchase. `env/bank_vault_2k.hdr` is the image-based lighting the
viewport uses.

The kit is released under **CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication)**,
which is what makes bundling and redistributing it here possible. In short: you
may copy, modify, distribute and use it, including commercially, without asking

Note on what is bundled
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Only the `glTF/` variant of the pack is included — the editor loads glTF, and
the Blender, FBX, OBJ, Godot and engine-project variants would add hundreds of
megabytes it never reads. The full pack, including the .blend sources and the
textures, is available from the author.

The HDRI in `env/` is **not** distributed with the editor and is not covered by
the dedication above. The one sitting there locally is a placeholder with no
recorded provenance, so it is gitignored rather than committed; a licensed
replacement will take its place. The editor starts without it — metals simply
render flat, since they take nearly all their brightness from the IBL.
